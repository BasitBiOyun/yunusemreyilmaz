# Language Room — Product Definition

Language Room is not a conventional portfolio page. It is an explorable personal workspace for Yunus Emre Yılmaz: English teacher, translator, product builder and media creator.

## Core experience

The visitor enters a real-time 3D study room. The room itself is navigation. Physical objects are meaningful shortcuts, while the computer is the main content portal. Clicking the computer moves the camera into the monitor and reveals a bespoke interface inside the screen instead of opening detached cards or side panels.

## Primary content

- Teaching: English teaching, YDT/YDS, reading, vocabulary and original materials.
- Translation: EN ↔ TR translation, editing, terminology and localization.
- Projects: Squad Index and Reflect & Shoot, with room for future projects.
- Media: YouTube and X/Twitter.
- About: professional identity, education, experience and future CV/contact content.

## Interaction principles

The room should feel discoverable, not confusing. The monitor is always the strongest visual target. Secondary objects may open a specific monitor section, but the experience remains spatial rather than modal-heavy. Escape/back always returns the visitor to the room.

Desktop receives the full cinematic experience. Mobile keeps the same art direction but may simplify camera movement and scene complexity when needed for usability and performance.
